Tiny Tots privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Tiny Tots is a learning game for young children. Your child's progress never leaves the device. The app shows optional ads in the grown-ups area only; this policy explains what that involves.

What we collect

We (the developer) collect nothing. Tiny Tots has no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it does not ask for permission to use location, camera, microphone or contacts. We never see, store, share or sell your child's progress or any personal information.

Advertising

Tiny Tots shows no ads while your child plays. Optional ads appear only in the grown-ups area, which is behind a grown-up check: a small banner, and a short video a grown-up can choose to watch to give the child a bonus (at most twice a day).

These ads are served by Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as directed to children (for the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for European and UK data-protection law), and is limited to ads rated suitable for general audiences (G). Ads are therefore never personalized, and Google does not use them for remarketing. The app does not use the device's advertising ID: that permission, and Android's Privacy Sandbox ad permissions, are removed from the app.

To serve and measure these ads and prevent fraud, Google's ads software receives information such as the device and app version, IP address, language and the ad interactions. Google handles this under its policies for child-directed ads: see https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431. The internet is used only to load these ads; every game works without it.

What stays on the device

To remember each child's progress, the app keeps small files on the device with stars, stickers, which numbers and letters have been learned, minutes and rounds played over the last week, and the settings chosen in the grown-ups area. If a grown-up adds more than one child, the app also keeps the name typed for each child (optional) and the animal face picked for them, so siblings can tell their games apart. These files are only used by the app on that device. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include these files in your own device backup, under Google's backup terms.

You can delete them at any time: in the grown-ups area, choose "Remove" next to a child or "Reset all progress", or uninstall the app.

Reminders

A grown-up can switch on an optional daily reminder in the grown-ups area. It is a notification set up and shown by the device itself; nothing is sent anywhere. On Android 13 and later the device asks for permission first. The app also re-arms the reminder after the device restarts. It stops after three reminders in a row go unanswered, and it can be switched off at any time.

Speech

Pip's voice uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device. The app only sends the words to be spoken to that engine; it records nothing.

Children

The app is made for children aged 2 to 5. We collect no information about children, so there is nothing to review or delete on our side. Settings, ads, time limits and links to system settings are all behind a grown-up check.

Changes

If this policy ever changes (for example, if optional features are added), the new version will be published here with a new date before the change reaches the app.
