About the game

Tiny Tots is a gentle, offline learning world for children aged 2 to 5, with Pip the chick as a friendly guide.

21 games that grow with your child

  • Numbers: Number Bubbles, Counting Train, Hungry Monster, Trace 123, Quick Look
  • Letters and sounds: Letter Bubbles, Trace ABC, Sound Blocks (build words from syllables), First Sounds (match pictures to their first letter)
  • Colors and shapes: Color Balloons, Shape Sorter, Finger Paint
  • Play and think: Animal Friends, Memory Match, Music Piano, Animal Band, Sing Along, Story Time
  • Everyday life: Feelings with Pip, Daily Routines (brush, wash, tidy), Off-screen Missions

Learns at your child's pace

Games get a little harder when your child is breezing through and gentler when they need help. New numbers and letters unlock as earlier ones are mastered.

An adventure to follow

An adventure map, friends to meet, eggs to hatch, stickers to collect and Pip's house to decorate keep little ones coming back.

Made for grown-ups too

  • Daily play-time limit and a bedtime wind-down
  • A weekly card with minutes played and a play-together idea
  • Progress for every number, letter, color, shape and animal
  • Calm mode with less movement; follows battery saver
  • Settings sit behind a grown-up check

Safe and offline

  • No ads while your child plays: optional ads appear only in the grown-ups area, behind a parent check
  • All games work offline; the internet is used only to load those optional ads
  • No accounts, no in-app purchases; ads are child-safe (rated G) and never personalized

13 languages

English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Português, Русский, हिन्दी, العربية, 中文, 日本語, Bahasa Indonesia, Italiano and Türkçe. Pip speaks using your device's text-to-speech voice.
