🐰 Pawzzle Pals – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026  ·  Developer: Ravi Dwivedi  ·  Contact: [EMAIL]

Summary

Pawzzle Pals is a puzzle game for all ages, including children. We (the developer) do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The only data that leaves the device is the limited technical data Google’s ad SDK processes to show ads (see "Advertising"). The app has no accounts, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and the game works fully offline. When the device is online, the app may show a small number of child-safe ads provided by Google AdMob (see "Advertising" below).

Advertising

When the device is connected to the internet, the app may occasionally show a full-screen ad between two levels (never during a puzzle, never in the first levels, and only a few times per session), or a short video that a grown-up chooses to start in the Closet, behind a grown-up check, in exchange for in-game treats. There are no banner ads. Without an internet connection no ads are shown.

  • Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Every ad request is marked as directed to children and under the age of consent, so ads are never personalised (no interest-based ads, no remarketing) and are limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences).
  • The app does not use or request the Android advertising ID, and does not use Android Privacy Sandbox ad APIs (Topics, Attribution).
  • To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad SDK automatically processes limited technical information: the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate a general location), interactions with the ad (for example, that an ad was shown or a video was watched), diagnostic information about the app and SDK, and the app set ID. This data is sent to Google encrypted (TLS). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Because every ad request is marked as coming from a user under the age of consent, Google serves only limited, non-personalised ads (also in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland) and no ad-consent pop-up is shown to children. If Google ever requires a consent choice, a grown-up can open it in the game's Settings under "Ad privacy choices" (behind the grown-up check).

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels completed, stars, treats, chosen pal, hats, garden theme, streak and settings) only on your device. This information never leaves your device, except through Android's own backup service if you have turned on device backup in your Google account settings. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only to load ads. The game itself never needs the internet.
  • Notifications (optional): only if you turn on the daily reminder. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.
  • Run at startup: used only to keep your daily reminder scheduled after the phone restarts.
  • Motion sensor: the background moves gently when the device is tilted. Sensor data is used live on the device and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Read aloud

An optional "Read aloud" setting (off by default, turned on behind a grown-up check) speaks the game's built-in words using a text-to-speech voice that is already installed on your phone. It runs entirely on the device: the game requests on-device speech only, never downloads or uses an online voice, and never passes on anything about you. If no voice is installed for the language, it stays silent.

Children

The app is designed to comply with the Google Play Families Policy and COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads are requested as child-directed (COPPA) and non-personalised, using an ad SDK that is self-certified under Google Play's Families Ads Program. Actions that leave the app or start a video (sharing a result, starting a rewarded video, ad privacy choices) are protected by a parental gate.

Sharing

If a grown-up chooses "Share", the app hands a short text (for example "🐰 Pawzzle Pals — Level 12 ⭐⭐⭐") to Android's share menu. We do not receive or see what is shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
