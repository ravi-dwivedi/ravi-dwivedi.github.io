About the game

Help your pals find the coziest spots in their garden! 🦊🌷

Pawzzle Pals is a calm, cute logic puzzle. Place one pal in every coloured garden – one in each row and column, and pals can't touch, not even at the corners. Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved with logic alone, no guessing.

  • 🧩 1,000 levels across magical worlds – boards grow from 5×5 to 9×9 – plus Expert puzzles
  • 💡 Smart hints explain the next step, and if you're stuck your pal gently points where to look
  • 🧘 Relax mode and no timers – play at your own pace
  • 🐾 50 pals to meet, one every 20 levels, from Common to Mythic
  • 🎭 Every pal has its own personality (playful, sleepy, shy, show-off, snacky or curious), dance, song, voice, favourite snack and secret move – plus little habits: the whale spouts, the frog croaks, the octopus changes colour…
  • 🤗 Play with your pal: pet, tickle, high-five, feed it by dragging a snack, draw a circle to make it spin, or shake the phone to make it dizzy
  • 💗 Give your pal a cute name and grow your friendship – heart blushes, starry eyes and a golden glow for best friends
  • 📖 Pal Book with stamps for every pal's snack, dance, secret and best friendship, plus a photo album of happy moments
  • 🙈 Hide & Seek, a Music Box with every pal's song and a Pal band, and a Photo Booth (saving needs a grown-up)
  • 📅 Daily Puzzle with streaks, a 7-day gift calendar, missions, a Sticker Book, treasure chests and gardener ranks
  • 🧭 A Weekly Path with a different helper pal every week
  • 🏡 Decorate My Garden with rides and throw a garden party where pals act just like themselves
  • 🎀 Dress your pals in hats, glasses and scarves, meet seasonal visitors and pick a buddy pal
  • 🌙 Bedtime sleepovers with a lullaby
  • 🌍 Plays offline in 18 languages, with big text, calm mode and read-aloud – and it's light on battery

Perfect for kids learning logical thinking, and for grown-ups who want a relaxing brain break.
