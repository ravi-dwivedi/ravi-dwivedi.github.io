About the game

Help Bunny and friends find the coziest spots in their garden! 🐰🌷

Pawzzle Pals is a calm, cute logic puzzle that's easy to learn and fun to master. Place one pal in every colored garden – but pals can't share a row or column, and they need personal space, so they can't touch, not even at the corners!

  • 🧩 Endless puzzles across magical worlds – boards grow from 5×5 to 9×9 as you improve
  • 💡 Smart hints that TEACH – your pal explains the next logical step
  • 🎯 Three new missions every day, with sweet rewards
  • 🏅 A Sticker Book full of achievements to collect
  • 🐼 10 pals to unlock: Bunny, Panda, Puppy, Penguin, Fox, Koala, Hamster, Chick, Froggy and Unicorn
  • 🎀 Pal Closet – dress your pal in bows, crowns, rainbows and more
  • 🌅 7 garden themes – from Meadow and Ocean to Forest and Starlight
  • 🌸 Gardens bloom with flowers when you solve them
  • 📅 A new Daily Puzzle every day – build your 🔥 streak and catch up on days you missed
  • 🎁 A daily gift from your pal
  • 🌙 The sky changes with the real time of day
  • 💾 Your puzzle is saved automatically – pick up right where you left off
  • ❤️ Gentle hearts system – no timers, no pressure
  • 🪨 Special gardens: rocky gardens, sleepy pals, golden gardens and a boss garden in every world
  • 🎁 Treasure chests full of surprises, gardener ranks to climb and combo sparkles
  • 🧘 Relax mode for little ones: no hearts to lose
  • 🏡 Decorate My Garden, follow a new Weekly Path, grow 💗 friendship with every pal and join seasonal parties
  • 📖 A cozy storybook: every world opens with a new chapter told by your pals
  • 🎵 Gentle music, cute pal voices and read-aloud for little ones
  • 🌍 Plays in 18 languages
  • 🔒 Only a few kid-safe ads, never personalised. No accounts. Plays offline. No in-app purchases.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved with logic alone – no guessing! Perfect for kids learning logical thinking, and for grown-ups who want a relaxing brain break.
