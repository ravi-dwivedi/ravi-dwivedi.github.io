Hi, I'm Ravi Dwivedi

Software engineer and indie Android developer, India

I have been building software for more than ten years, across payments, travel and e-commerce, using Java, C++, Python, SQL and Android. In my spare time I make small, calm games for kids and families.

Every game I make follows the same simple rules: no accounts, no in-app purchases, progress stays on your device, and only a few child-safe, non-personalised ads. I want grown-ups to feel good about handing their phone to a child.
